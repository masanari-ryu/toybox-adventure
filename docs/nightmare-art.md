# Nightmare ending illustrations

Generated using the built-in image generation tool, with `public/assets/homecoming-story.png` as the style and protagonist reference. The discarded four-panel draft is not used in the game.

Final prompt set:
- Wake: Standalone wide cinematic ending illustration matching the reference's warm detailed storybook aesthetic. Same androgynous child, tousled brown hair, mint sweater and navy pants, waking in bed with sweat beads and a startled relieved expression. Cozy morning bedroom, rocket posters, treasured toybox. No text, collage or low-poly rendering.
- Thanks: Standalone wide illustration in the same style and with the same child kneeling by the open wooden star-and-rocket toybox, hand over heart, looking gratefully at teddy, robot and train. Detailed warm morning bedroom. No text or speech bubbles.
- Friends: Standalone wide illustration at the school courtyard. The same child, now wearing a yellow backpack, happily invites a friendly gender-ambiguous slightly chubby child with round cheeks, softly rounded short cropped hair, plain blue shirt, shorts and backpack. Expressive faces and natural hands, richly detailed sunny school and flowers. No text or speech bubbles.

Final assets:
- `public/assets/nightmare-wake.webp`
- `public/assets/nightmare-thanks.webp`
- `public/assets/nightmare-friends.webp`

PNG originals remain in the image generation output directory. WebP exports retain original dimensions at quality 90. The school journey scene uses the existing illustration. Captions and Japanese speech timing remain independent of the artwork.

Revision: Built-in image editing preserved the scene and right protagonist, softened the friend’s facial features and short hair to avoid a clearly gendered appearance, and removed printed patterns from both main children’s clothes. Natural fabric texture remains.
